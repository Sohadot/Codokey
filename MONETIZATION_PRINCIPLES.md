# Monetization Principles

Revenue philosophy for the Codokey sovereign digital asset.

## Core Principle

Codokey monetizes through **strategic asset value** — not through SaaS subscriptions, fake product sales, or engagement funnels.

The asset is worth what a buyer will pay for category definition, reference infrastructure, brand authority, and documentation corpus — not what a monthly subscription generates.

## Permitted Revenue Paths

### 1. Strategic Acquisition

Full transfer of brand, domain, content, documentation, and reference model IP to an acquiring organization.

### 2. Licensing

- Reference model citation licensing for enterprise policy documents
- Scorecard branding for internal governance programs
- Documentation corpus licensing for training and compliance

### 3. Institutional Partnership

- Protocol co-extension with platform vendors
- Category co-development with industry bodies
- Reference adoption programs with governance consultancies

### 4. Domain & Brand Value

Appreciation of codokey.com as category-defining domain in AI Code Access Governance.

## Prohibited Revenue Paths

- SaaS subscription without an actual product
- Fake "enterprise tier" pricing pages
- Newsletter monetization as primary strategy
- Affiliate marketing for security tools
- Certification fees without certification authority
- Lead generation sold to third parties
- Advertising on reference documentation pages

## Pricing Philosophy

No public pricing. Strategic assets are priced through negotiation, not self-serve checkout.

Inquiry language must remain restrained:

> Serious strategic acquisition, licensing, or institutional partnership inquiries may be considered.

Not:

> "Book a demo" / "Talk to sales" / "Limited time offer"

## Asset Value Drivers

1. **Category ownership** — first-mover in AI Code Access Governance definition
2. **Citation density** — how often the reference model is cited in policies and docs
3. **Documentation depth** — charter, protocol, scorecard, buyer brief completeness
4. **Technical durability** — static, GitHub Pages ready, no dependency debt
5. **Brand clarity** — Codokey name maps cleanly to code + key governance

## Non-Goals

- Maximize page views through blog content
- Build email list for marketing automation
- Create urgency through countdown timers
- Present as funded startup seeking users
