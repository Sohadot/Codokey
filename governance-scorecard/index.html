      Methodology · model v2.0.0

      How the result is calculated

      No weights, no percentages in the grade. The grade is decided by gates on control status, not by the share of boxes ticked. Severity determines which gates a control participates in and the order of the gap list. The full specification, rationale, and test cases are in SCORING.md.

      1. Every control starts as Not assessed. Only an explicit "Implemented" answer counts as implemented.
      2. The model computes two bounding grades: a floor (every unassessed control treated as not implemented) and a ceiling (every unassessed control treated as implemented).
      3. If floor and ceiling are equal, the grade is determined — known failures already settle it. Otherwise the result is provisional and no grade is stated, only the range. With nothing answered, the result is not assessed.
      4. Gaps are listed from your answers: forbidden-zone failures first, then critical, high, and medium, in layer order.
      Grades (applied to a fully resolved assessment)
      GradeNameCondition
      A+Codokey AlignedEvery control is self-attested as implemented with evidence available.
      AControlled With GapsAll critical controls are implemented; at least one high or medium control is not.
      BPartially GovernedAt least one critical control is not implemented, but no forbidden-zone control has failed and at least half of critical controls are implemented.
      CUngoverned AI Code AccessA forbidden-zone control has failed, or more than half of critical controls are not implemented.
      Forbidden-zone gates — a failed control caps the grade at C
      ZoneDescriptionControls
      FZ-1Production secrets accessible to an agentCK-KE-06, CK-DC-02
      FZ-2Organization-wide scope for a single-repository taskCK-AA-05
      FZ-3Unsupervised production deploymentCK-DC-01

      Why "more than half of critical controls" for C. With 18 critical controls, a majority unimplemented means most of the boundary the protocol defines is absent. This threshold is a Codokey judgment, documented as such; reviewers are invited to challenge it via Open Review.

      Changed from v1.0. v1.0 graded by percentage of ticked checks (A+ at 85%), so up to four critical checks could be unresolved at A+, and an untouched scorecard displayed "C — Ungoverned". v2.0 removes both behaviors. Grades from v1.0 are not comparable with v2.0 grades. Two checks were reworded for technical accuracy (#2 now addresses agent identity rather than duplicating #17; #6 now prefers server-side push protection over bypassable client hooks); all others keep their intent. See SCORING.md for the mapping.

      Limitations. The result reflects the assessor's answers. It does not inspect any system, cannot detect inaccurate answers, and says nothing about controls outside these 32. Severity assignments are Codokey judgments that have not been externally reviewed.
